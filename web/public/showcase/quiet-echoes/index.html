Why search for quieter echoes?
Autocorrelation describes idealized alignment of a pulse with delayed copies. Lower total sidelobe energy is a useful signal-design objective and a difficult finite spin optimization problem.
A precise, limited claim
This benchmark does not score noise, Doppler, cross-correlation or hardware, and does not establish real receiver performance. One improved sequence would not prove the global optimum or solve an asymptotic conjecture.
