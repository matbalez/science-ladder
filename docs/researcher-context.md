# Researchers to know

Challenges may include a short, optional list of researchers whose published work
connects to the scientific question. The purpose is educational context and
possible community outreach: someone may wish to introduce the challenge and ask
whether a researcher would like to follow progress.

Being listed is not sponsorship, endorsement, an advisory role, or confirmed
interest. The public section states this explicitly. It contains names, public
academic profile links, a brief explanation of relevance, and a cited publication.
It does not collect private contact information or send messages.

Prefer two or three people with direct, source-backed connections to the problem.
Preserve the complete authorship of cited work even when only selected researchers
have individual cards. Do not infer a current title, affiliation or willingness to
participate from authorship. A later claim of participation needs actual evidence;
this feature only provides suggestions with unconfirmed interest.

Editors maintain this context in the review interface. Each update records its
author, time and public rationale. Clearing a list also creates a revision, so
previous context remains auditable. This is presentation metadata outside the
immutable scientific manifest: updating it does not change the challenge lock,
metric, verifier, deadline, milestones, accepted artifacts or receipt history.
